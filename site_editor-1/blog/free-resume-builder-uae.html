Getting a job in Dubai or anywhere in the Gulf starts with one thing — a CV that gets past the recruiter's 6-second scan. Most applicants send generic templates that look identical to thousands of others. This guide shows you exactly how to build a professional, ATS-friendly CV using EggyPDF's free Resume Builder, with no sign up and no hidden fees.

What Makes a UAE CV Different

The Gulf job market has specific expectations that differ from Western countries. UAE employers and recruiters typically expect:

  • A professional photo — unlike the UK or US, a headshot is standard and expected in the UAE
  • Nationality and visa status — employers need to know if you require visa sponsorship
  • Concise format — 1 to 2 pages maximum, no lengthy personal statements
  • Quantified achievements — numbers and percentages rather than vague descriptions
  • ATS compatibility — many Gulf companies use applicant tracking software that filters CVs before a human reads them
💡 Key Insight: Over 75% of CVs sent to large Gulf companies are filtered by ATS software before a recruiter ever sees them. A poorly formatted CV gets rejected automatically, no matter how qualified you are.

Step 2 — Fill in Your Contact Information

Start with the basics. In the UAE context, include:

  • Full name as it appears on your passport
  • UAE mobile number starting with +971
  • Professional email address (avoid nicknames)
  • Current location — e.g. Dubai, UAE or Sharjah, UAE
  • LinkedIn profile URL if you have one

Do not include your date of birth, marital status, or religion — these are not required and may create bias.

Step 3 — Write a Strong Professional Summary

The summary sits at the top of your CV and is the first thing a recruiter reads. Keep it to 2-3 sentences. Use the AI Summary Generator in EggyPDF to get 4 options tailored to your job title — then pick the one that matches your experience and edit it to add specific details.

💡 Pro Tip: Include your years of experience, your main skill area, and one specific achievement in your summary. Example: "Web Developer with 5 years of experience building e-commerce platforms. Reduced page load times by 40% at previous employer, increasing sales conversions by 15%."

Step 4 — Add Your Work Experience

List your most recent job first. For each role, include the job title, company name, location, and dates. Use the AI Bullet Points Generator to create achievement-focused bullet points rather than task descriptions.

The difference between a weak and strong bullet point:

  • Weak: Responsible for managing social media accounts
  • Strong: Grew Instagram followers by 12,000 in 6 months through targeted content strategy, increasing lead generation by 35%

Step 5 — Add Education and Skills

List your highest qualification first. Include the degree name, institution, country, and graduation year. For skills, add both technical skills (software, languages, tools) and relevant soft skills. The Resume Builder displays these as clean pill boxes that are easy to scan.

Step 6 — Upload Your Photo

A professional headshot is important in the UAE. Use a plain background, dress professionally, and make sure your face is clearly visible. The Resume Builder lets you upload and reposition your photo to fit perfectly in the circle frame.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using a generic objective statement instead of a targeted summary
  • Listing job duties instead of achievements
  • Including irrelevant experience from 10+ years ago
  • Using fancy fonts, tables, or columns that break ATS scanning
  • Sending the same CV to every job without tailoring the summary

Your CV is your first impression. Spend 30 minutes building it properly using this guide and you will stand out from the majority of applicants who submit generic, unformatted resumes.
