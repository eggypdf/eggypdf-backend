CV Format for Dubai Jobs: What Employers Actually Want

📅 June 2026 ⏱️ 5 min read ✍️ Ahmad — Founder, EggyPDF
← Back to Blog

EggyPDF's free Resume Builder is designed specifically for UAE and Dubai job seekers. The UAE job market is one of the most competitive in the world. Dubai alone receives millions of job applications every year from candidates across South Asia, Southeast Asia, Europe, and Africa. Your CV format can make or break your application before a recruiter reads a single word.

The Standard Dubai CV Format

Unlike the US and UK where CVs are often 1 page, Dubai employers typically expect 1-2 pages with these sections in order:

  1. Personal Information and Contact Details
  2. Professional Summary
  3. Work Experience
  4. Education
  5. Skills
  6. Languages

Should You Include a Photo?

Yes. Unlike Western countries where photos on CVs are discouraged, UAE employers generally expect a professional headshot. Use a recent photo with a neutral or light background, professional attire, and a confident expression. Do not use a selfie or casual photo.

Nationality and Visa Status

Include your nationality and current visa status on your UAE CV. Employers need to know if you are already in the UAE on a valid visa, on a visit visa, or applying from abroad. For UAE nationals, mention this clearly as many roles have Emiratisation quotas.

What Not to Include

  • Salary expectations (unless the job ad specifically requests this)
  • References (state "available on request" instead)
  • Irrelevant hobbies or personal interests
  • More than 15 years of work history
  • Generic objective statements

Language Skills Matter More Here

Arabic proficiency significantly increases your chances across many sectors in the UAE — government, retail, real estate, and customer-facing roles. If you speak Arabic, Tagalog, Hindi, or Urdu in addition to English, list all of them. The UAE is a multilingual market and language skills are genuinely valued.
