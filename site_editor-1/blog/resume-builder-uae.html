How to Build a Professional CV for UAE Jobs in 2025

EggyPDF's free Resume Builder was created to help job seekers in the UAE build professional CVs without needing a designer. Finding a job in the UAE is competitive. With thousands of applicants for every position, your CV is your first impression — and in most cases, it is the only thing standing between you and an interview. Here is exactly what Dubai and UAE employers expect.

UAE CV Format: The Basics

A standard UAE CV follows a specific structure that differs slightly from European or American formats:

  • Length: 1-2 pages maximum. Recruiters spend an average of 7 seconds on a first scan.
  • Photo: A professional headshot is expected in the UAE, unlike in some Western countries.
  • Personal details: Include your nationality and visa status. Many employers in the UAE require this.
  • Contact info: Use a UAE mobile number if you have one. Include your LinkedIn profile.

What Every UAE CV Must Include

  1. Professional Summary — 3-4 sentences at the top. State your role, years of experience, and one key achievement.
  2. Work Experience — List in reverse chronological order. Include company name, location, dates, and 3-4 bullet points with measurable results.
  3. Education — Degree, institution, country, and graduation year.
  4. Skills — Keep this relevant. List technical skills specific to your industry.
  5. Languages — Arabic proficiency is a significant advantage in the UAE market.

ATS-Friendly Formatting

Most large companies in the UAE use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter CVs before a human ever reads them. To pass ATS screening:

  • Use standard section headings: Work Experience, Education, Skills
  • Avoid tables, columns, text boxes, and graphics in the main body
  • Use standard fonts like Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman
  • Include keywords from the job description naturally in your text
  • Save as PDF, not Word, to preserve formatting

Common Mistakes UAE Job Seekers Make

  • Generic objective statements — Replace "seeking a challenging position" with a specific professional summary
  • No metrics — "Managed a team" is weak. "Managed a team of 8, increasing sales by 32%" is strong
  • Outdated contact details — Always use your current email and phone number
  • Poor PDF formatting — What looks great in Word often breaks when saved as PDF. Use a dedicated builder
