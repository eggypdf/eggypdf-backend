If you have ever tried to email a PDF and been told the file is too large, you are not alone. PDFs can quickly become bloated — especially when they contain images, scanned documents, or multiple pages. The good news is that compressing a PDF is simple and completely free.

Why PDF Files Get Too Large

PDF files grow in size because of:

  • High-resolution images embedded in the document
  • Scanned pages saved at maximum DPI
  • Embedded fonts that are not optimized
  • Multiple pages with complex graphics

Most email services have a 10MB attachment limit. WhatsApp limits documents to 100MB. For job applications in the UAE, recruiters prefer CVs under 2MB so they load quickly on mobile.

How Much Can You Compress a PDF?

Results depend on the content of your PDF:

  • Image-heavy PDFs — typically 60-80% size reduction
  • Text-only PDFs — 20-40% size reduction
  • Scanned documents — 40-70% size reduction

Tips for Smaller PDFs

Beyond compression, you can keep your PDFs small from the start:

  • Save images as JPG instead of PNG before inserting them into documents
  • Use 150 DPI instead of 300 DPI for scanned documents that will only be read on screen
  • Remove unnecessary pages before saving
  • Use a PDF-native font instead of embedding custom fonts when possible
